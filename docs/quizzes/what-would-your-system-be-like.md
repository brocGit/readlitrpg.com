## Results

### The Snarky Announcer

**Your System has opinions, and an audience.**

It narrates your worst moments with relish, hands out achievements for embarrassing yourself, and never, ever lets anything go. You'd be lost without it, and it knows.

- **You'll love:** Snarky Systems · Humor · Rule of Cool
- **Watch out for:** Silent, joyless Systems.
- **Starting profile:** humor → 9 · rule_of_cool ≥ 6 · #snarky-system #humorous
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Snarky Announcer. It has opinions, and an audience. What would yours be?"

### The Helpful Guide

**Your System wants you to make it.**

It explains things, cheers you on, and occasionally bends a rule when you really need it. You like your stories with a warm heart, and so does your System.

- **You'll love:** A warm tone · Rootable MCs · Low drama
- **Watch out for:** Cruel worlds where nobody is safe.
- **Starting profile:** tone → 8, danger → 3 · low_drama ≥ 6, rootable_mc ≥ 6 · #heroic #cozy
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Helpful Guide. It wants me to make it. What would yours be?"

### The Cold Machine

**Numbers. Rules. Exact percentages.**

Your System doesn't care about you, and that's fine, because you care about the math. Every modifier is visible, every rule is absolute, and every exploit is fair game.

- **You'll love:** Heavy crunch · Hard rules · Build payoff
- **Watch out for:** Handwavy Systems that change the rules whenever it's convenient.
- **Starting profile:** crunch → 9, rigour → 9, strategy → 7 · system_consistency ≥ 8, build_payoff ≥ 7 · #stats
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Cold Machine: numbers, rules, exact percentages. What would yours be?"

### The Cruel Game Master

**It's watching, and it is not on your side.**

Your System thinks your suffering is excellent content. Impossible quests, brutal penalties and an audience that bets on your death. You like survival that's earned and stakes that are real.

- **You'll love:** High stakes · Earned power · Dark tone
- **Watch out for:** Plot armor thick enough to stop a dragon.
- **Starting profile:** danger → 9, tone → 2 · earned_power ≥ 7 · #grimdark #system-apocalypse
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Cruel Game Master. It is not on my side. What would yours be?"

### The Glitchy Mess

**Held together with duct tape and hope.**

Your System is buggy, and you're the one who found the bug. You love an MC who breaks the game in ways the designers never imagined, and gets away with it.

- **You'll love:** System exploits · Cheat abilities · Rule of Cool
- **Watch out for:** Flawless Systems with nothing to break.
- **Starting profile:** humor → 6, strategy → 7 · rule_of_cool ≥ 7 · #system-exploitation #cheat-ability
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Glitchy Mess, and I found the bug. What would yours be?"

### The Silent Mystery

**It never explains. It definitely has an agenda.**

Your System barely speaks, and when it does, it raises more questions than it answers. You want to know what it really is, and you'll read ten books to find out.

- **You'll love:** System mysteries · Deep lore · Tightly plotted arcs
- **Watch out for:** Worlds with no secrets left to find.
- **Starting profile:** lore → 9, plot_structure → 7 · system_consistency ≥ 6 · #system-mystery #hidden-system
- **Share text:** "My System would be The Silent Mystery. It never explains. What would yours be?"
