## Results

### Path of Black Flame

**Burn hotter than anything that stands in your way.**

Fire and destruction, woven together into one of the most feared Paths there is. You don't do subtle. You do relentless: every obstacle is fuel, every setback makes the fire hotter, and people who underestimate you tend to regret it very quickly.

- **You'll love:** Relentless MCs · Hype moments · Big power jumps · High stakes
- **Watch out for:** Cozy stories where nothing is on fire.
- **Starting profile:** combat → 8, danger → 7, power_fantasy → 6 · hype ≥ 8, number_go_up ≥ 7 · #progression-fantasy #ranks-tiers
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of Black Flame. I burn hotter than anything in my way. What's yours?"

### Path of the Endless Sword

**Everything can be cut.**

Sword madra, sharpened to a single purpose. You're blunt, direct and absolutely relentless, and you'd rather cut through a problem than talk around it. Your loyalty is just as sharp as your edge, even if you'd never say so out loud.

- **You'll love:** Weapon masters · Earned power · Blunt characters · Training arcs
- **Watch out for:** Endless politics and scheming.
- **Starting profile:** combat → 8, strategy → 4 · earned_power ≥ 8, hype ≥ 7 · #class-warrior #training-arcs #ranks-tiers
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of the Endless Sword. Everything can be cut. What's yours?"

### Path of the White Fox

**Reality is more of a suggestion.**

Dream madra and illusions, from a Path founded by a sacred white fox. You win with misdirection, patience and the look on someone's face when they realize the thing they were fighting was never there.

- **You'll love:** Clever MCs · Mind games · Magic with rules · Lore
- **Watch out for:** Brute-force stories.
- **Starting profile:** strategy → 8, lore → 6 · competent_mc ≥ 7, rule_of_cool ≥ 6 · #clever-mc #progression-fantasy
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of the White Fox. Reality is more of a suggestion. What's yours?"

### Path of Heaven's Glory

**Light, as a weapon.**

Burning light madra, taught at a school on the mountain. You like structure: clear ranks, real teachers and a proper way of doing things. You climb by discipline, and your techniques shine bright enough to hurt.

- **You'll love:** Academy stories · Clear power systems · Heroic tone
- **Watch out for:** Lawless chaos with no rules.
- **Starting profile:** rigour → 7, tone → 6 · system_consistency ≥ 7 · #academy #class-mage #ranks-tiers
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of Heaven's Glory. Light, as a weapon. What's yours?"

### Path of the Stellar Spear

**Strike first. Strike true. Remember everything.**

The signature spear art of a proud clan. You're precise, ambitious and you never, ever forget a slight. Your rivals should know that every defeat you suffer is just the first chapter of your comeback.

- **You'll love:** Rivalries · Revenge arcs · Precise fighters · Dark tone
- **Watch out for:** MCs who forgive too easily.
- **Starting profile:** danger → 7, morality → 6, tone → 4 · hype ≥ 7 · #revenge #class-warrior
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of the Stellar Spear. Strike first, strike true, remember everything. What's yours?"

### Path of the Fisherman

**Why fight it when you can build something better?**

Force madra with a gentle pull, perfect for soulsmithing. You're the crafter: the one who turns scraps into constructs and makes everyone else's gear actually work. You're cranky about it, sure. You're also indispensable.

- **You'll love:** Crafting · Soulsmithing and item creation · Grumpy mentors · Build payoff
- **Watch out for:** Stories that are all fighting and no making.
- **Starting profile:** combat → 4, strategy → 7 · build_payoff ≥ 8 · #crafting #teaching
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is the Path of the Fisherman. Why fight it when you can build something better? What's yours?"

### Pure Madra (Heart of Twin Stars)

**They called you Unsouled. They were wrong.**

You started with the weakest madra there is and no Path at all, so you made your own advantages: a split core, a technique that disrupts an enemy's madra, and a plan for every situation. Nobody out-prepares you, and nobody works harder.

- **You'll love:** Underdogs · Clever MCs · Earned power · Weak to strong
- **Watch out for:** MCs born with every advantage.
- **Starting profile:** power_fantasy → 2, strategy → 9 · earned_power ≥ 9, competent_mc ≥ 8 · #weak-to-strong #clever-mc #progression-fantasy
- **Share text:** "My Cradle Path is Pure Madra. They called me Unsouled. They were wrong. What's yours?"
