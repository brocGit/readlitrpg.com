## Results

### Carl

**No pants. No plan. No quit.**

You walked into the end of the world in your boxers and decided that was a them problem. You're sarcastic, stubborn and fiercely protective of your people, and your favorite weapon is whatever the dungeon didn't expect you to use: the terrain, a trap, something that goes boom. The showrunners are learning to regret you.

- **You'll love:** Dark humor · Clever improvisation · Protective MCs · Sticking it to the system
- **Watch out for:** Heroes who follow the rules they were handed.
- **Starting profile:** humor → 8, danger → 7, strategy → 7 · rootable_mc ≥ 8, competent_mc ≥ 7 · #dungeon-crawler #dark-humor #system-exploitation
- **Share text:** "I got Carl on the Dungeon Crawler Carl quiz. No pants, no plan, no quit. Who are you?"

### Princess Donut

**The galaxy is watching, and you look fabulous.**

You were born for the spotlight and you know it. You're dramatic, magnificent and far sharper than you let on, and you'd fight the whole dungeon for the people you've decided are yours. You also have strong opinions about your party's name.

- **You'll love:** Rule of Cool · Big personalities · Found family · Spectacle
- **Watch out for:** Quiet, low-drama stories where nobody gets a grand entrance.
- **Starting profile:** humor → 8, ensemble → 7 · rule_of_cool ≥ 8, hype ≥ 7 · #humorous #party-focused #class-mage
- **Share text:** "I got Princess Donut on the Dungeon Crawler Carl quiz. The galaxy is watching, and I look fabulous. Who are you?"

### Mordecai

**Gruff guide. Knows every rule. Cares more than he admits.**

You've seen it all, you've read every rule, and you have absolutely no patience for people who haven't. You'll complain the whole time you're keeping everyone alive, but you'll keep them alive. Every good party needs someone who actually knows how this works.

- **You'll love:** Mentors · Knowing the rules · Crunchy systems · Competent characters
- **Watch out for:** MCs who never read the tooltip.
- **Starting profile:** strategy → 8, crunch → 7, lore → 7 · competent_mc ≥ 8, system_consistency ≥ 7 · #teaching #clever-mc
- **Share text:** "I got Mordecai on the Dungeon Crawler Carl quiz. Gruff, knows every rule, cares more than I admit. Who are you?"

### Mongo

**Screech first. Ask questions never.**

You're pure enthusiasm with teeth. Point you at a problem and you'll charge it, preferably at full volume, then look for snacks. You're loyal to the bone, and anyone who threatens your people finds that out the hard way.

- **You'll love:** Nonstop action · Animal companions · Loyalty · Fights that don't need a plan
- **Watch out for:** Slow chapters with no one to bite.
- **Starting profile:** combat → 9, pacing → 8, humor → 7 · rule_of_cool ≥ 7 · #animal-companion #monster-mc
- **Share text:** "I got Mongo on the Dungeon Crawler Carl quiz. Screech first, ask questions never. Who are you?"

### Imani

**Someone has to keep everyone alive. It's you.**

You came in for a night shift, ended up in a dungeon, and never stopped taking care of people. While everyone else panics, you're counting heads, patching wounds and making a plan for tomorrow. The dungeon is brutal; your people are still standing because of you.

- **You'll love:** Found family · Healers and support roles · Caregivers who never quit · Hope under pressure
- **Watch out for:** Lone-wolf MCs who leave people behind.
- **Starting profile:** ensemble → 9, tone → 6 · party_chemistry ≥ 8, rootable_mc ≥ 7 · #found-family #support-class-mc #class-healer
- **Share text:** "I got Imani on the Dungeon Crawler Carl quiz. Someone has to keep everyone alive. Who are you?"

### Elle

**Ninety-nine years old. Unimpressed by the apocalypse.**

Ninety-nine years in, the end of the world is just one more thing to sit through. You've seen everything, you're not easily rattled, and you'd like everyone to stop fussing. Readers love you because you're a reminder that the people everyone overlooks are still in this story.

- **You'll love:** Older heroes · Underestimated elders · Dry humor · Characters who refuse to be rushed
