# Runbook: scheduled jobs

Every job is registered in `packages/core/src/scheduler/index.ts` (`JOBS`), has a handler in `apps/jobs/src/jobs/`, and a line here. The heartbeat runs every 5 minutes, queues due jobs on `rlr-jobs`, and the jobs Worker runs them. A run that fails is retried (up to 3 times, with backoff), then lands in the dead-letter queue, which opens an Owner Inbox item.

Admin → Automation shows each job's schedule and history, and can pause, resume, re-time or run any job now.

| Job | When (UTC) | What it does | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| `backup.export` | 03:00 daily | Exports every table in `BACKUP_TABLES` as NDJSON with a checksummed manifest to `rlr-backups` (`d1/daily/<date>/`, plus `d1/monthly/` on the 1st) | Run it again from Automation. Two misses in a row: check R2 and the job error. D1 Time Travel still covers 30 days |
| `audit.verify` | 04:15 daily | Recomputes the audit hash chain | A break opens a priority-100 inbox item naming the row. Treat it as a security incident: compare against the last backup's `audit_log` part |
| `retention.purge` | 05:30 daily | Deletes expired sessions, sign-in tokens and rate counters; job history over 90 days; anonymous quiz takes over 90 days; the email send log over 90 days; subscriptions never confirmed within 7 days (and addresses left with no consent); export files past their link's life; authors' pasted text a week after it's read, sent author notices after 30 days and emailed change notes after 90; news tips 90 days after they're reported or rejected; audit rows over 7 years | Safe to re-run. Nothing depends on it except table size |
| `taxonomy.sync` | hourly at :07 | Loads `data/taxonomy.yaml` into the `tags` table when its hash has changed (a deploy with taxonomy edits) | Admin → Taxonomy → *Sync now*. Tags are only ever added or updated, never deleted |
| `catalog.enrich` | every 15 minutes | Looks up to 25 books in Open Library (and Google Books if `GOOGLE_BOOKS_API_KEY` is set). A match confirms the book and adds page count and first publication date | Errors are retried after `enrich.retry_days`. A book page's *Look it up again* forces a retry. If every lookup errors, check the Worker's outbound access to openlibrary.org |
| `editorial.queue` | every 15 minutes | Queues work for editorial runs: every unclassified book, each open "Possible duplicate" without a run's verdict, and seeds Open Library couldn't confirm (not retried within 30 days) | Safe to re-run: each cause has at most one open item. Admin → Editorial → *Look for new work now* does the same |
| `editorial.watchdog` | hourly at :23 | Returns expired claims to the queue (an item is expired after three unanswered claims), closes runs still "running" after 12 hours, and opens an `editorial_stale` inbox item when work has waited longer than `editorial.stale_hours` with no successful run | Check the routines and the environment's tokens ([editorial-runs.md](editorial-runs.md)) |
| `editorial.citations` | every 10 minutes | Fetches the pages research runs cited (up to 10 proposals a run). A page showing the title and an author's full name confirms the seed and applies the cited facts | Network errors are retried three times, then the proposal is marked unverified and the seed is researched again later |
| `vectors.update` | hourly at :40 | Embeds up to `embed.batch_size` new or changed books with Workers AI, then flags near-identical books by one author as possible duplicates | Does nothing until `CF_ACCOUNT_ID` and `CF_API_TOKEN` are set on the jobs Worker. A 401/403 means the token lacks the Workers AI permission |
| `media.covers` | every 15 minutes | Looks for a licensed cover for up to 10 books without one: Open Library by the cover id enrichment recorded, then by ISBN. A found cover is checked, kept in `rlr-private` and processed straight away. Each book is tried again after 30 days | Nothing breaks without it: pages show a generated placeholder. A book page's *Look for an Open Library cover* retries one book now. If every lookup fails, check the Worker's outbound access to covers.openlibrary.org and archive.org |
| `media.process` | every 5 minutes, and straight away after an upload | Re-encodes pending images through the Images binding into WebP variants in `rlr-media` (160, 320 and 640 wide), attaches covers to their books and queues an editorial image review. An upload always replaces a fetched cover | A failed image is marked rejected with the reason (shown on the admin book page); upload it again. If every image fails, check that Cloudflare Images is enabled for the account |
| `og.render` | every 15 minutes, and straight away when a quiz is published | Draws link-preview PNGs into `rlr-media`: the site card, the 12 reader classes, every result of each live quiz, and up to 15 changed books per run | Pages point at the PNGs by key, so a missed run only means a preview appears later. Bump `OG_VERSION` in `packages/core/src/media/og.ts` to redraw everything after a layout change |
| `stats.rollup` | hourly at :17 | Copies the last two days of page views from Analytics Engine into `page_views_daily` and `referrers_daily` (Admin → Traffic), and ad delivery (served, viewable, clicks) into `campaign_stats_daily` (Admin → Ads) | Does nothing until `CF_ACCOUNT_ID` and `CF_API_TOKEN` are set on the jobs Worker. A 401/403 means the token lacks *Account Analytics · Read*. Safe to re-run: each day's totals are replaced, not added |
| `quiz.announce` | hourly at :09 | Opens a `quiz_ready` inbox item for each quiz that shipped in the code without a publish decision. The heartbeat publishes it once `quiz.auto_publish_hours` (48) have passed, unless it was retired in Admin → Quizzes | Safe to re-run: one item per quiz, ever. To stop a quiz going live, retire it in Admin → Quizzes, or set the hours to 0 so every quiz waits for you |
| `match.model_build` | hourly at :50 | Rebuilds the match feature matrix from published books and stores it in KV. The version comes from the content, so an unchanged catalog writes nothing; the site picks up a new version within a minute | Admin → Match shows the current version and can rebuild now or roll back. A rollback pins the model, so this job skips until someone rebuilds by hand |
| `email.welcome` | every 5 minutes | Sends due welcome-sequence steps (QUIZZES §3.4): E1 the reading list right after confirmation, E2 on day 2, E3 on day 5, E4 on day 9; "just the list" readers get E1 only. A step whose ask is already done is skipped, and a reader with no active reading-list or weekly consent leaves the sequence | Safe to re-run: each step advances once. Marketing mail waits (with one inbox item) until `email.postal_address` is set in production |
| `email.release_alerts` | every 10 minutes, 11:00–13:50 | One bundled email per reader on `release_alerts`: follows set to "on release day" with a release today, and new books for saved searches set to instant. A cursor in KV (`email:release_alerts:<day>`) makes later runs continue where the last stopped | A missed day isn't resent. Delete the day's KV cursor to run it again from the start (readers may get a second email) |
| `email.digest_build` | Thursday 09:00 | Freezes this week's Patch Notes issue: books published in the last 7 days and the quiz of the week | Run it again from Automation; it does nothing if the week's issue exists |
| `email.digest_send` | every 5 minutes, Friday–Saturday 13:00–23:55 | From Friday 13:00 builds each reader's issue (out this week from follows, new matches, saved searches, coming soon) and queues it, `email.digest_chunk` readers a run. Stops for `flags.newsletter_send`, `email.daily_cap` and the circuit breaker (`email.circuit.*`, judged after 200 sends), which pauses the issue and opens an inbox item | A paused issue stays paused: check bounces and complaints in SES first, then set the issue back to `sending` in D1 to continue from its cursor |
| `exports.build` | every 10 minutes | Builds up to 3 queued "export my data" files into `rlr-private` and emails each reader a link (valid 7 days, signed-in owner only) | Safe to re-run. An export for a deleted account is marked expired |
| `library.import` | every 5 minutes | Matches up to 160 rows of queued Goodreads / StoryGraph imports to published books (ISBN, then title and author) and turns them into book marks; the rows are deleted when an import finishes | Safe to re-run. Unmatched rows are counted, not kept |
| `authors.notices` | every 5 minutes | Emails authors about decisions recorded in the `author_notices` outbox: a listing published or not, a verification passed or refused, a claim decided, a protected change applied, drafts ready from a paste, a new team member (to owners) | Safe to re-run: each notice is marked sent. A notice whose profile has no active owner is marked sent without an email |
| `authors.change_digest` | every 15 minutes, 17:00–18:45 | From 17:00 UTC, one email per author profile listing what others (the owner, editorial runs, book databases) changed on their books before 17:00; 40 profiles a run | Safe to re-run: sent notes are stamped. Changes after 17:00 go in tomorrow's email |
| `release.confirm_asks` | 15:00, 15:20, 15:40 | Asks the owners of each published book with a release 14 or 3 days out "Still on for this date?", with a signed link (21 days) to confirm, change or delay it. Each release, stage and date is asked once | Safe to re-run. A missed day isn't asked again; the dashboard's to-do list still shows unanswered asks |
| `release.rollover` | hourly at :05 | Marks dated releases as released once their day has come (US dates by New York time) | Safe to re-run |
| `news.from_catalog` | hourly at :12 | Turns the last three hours of catalog changes into news tips: new dated releases (announcements), date changes, cancellations and series marked complete. One tip per cause | Safe to re-run: tips are deduplicated. A missed hour is covered by the next run's overlap |
| `news.briefs` | every 10 minutes | Fetches the pages cited by the morning run's news briefs (never Amazon, Audible, Royal Road or Goodreads). A brief whose page names its book, series or author publishes as a news post when `news.auto_publish_briefs` is on; anything else goes to the inbox as `news_brief` | Network errors are retried three times, then the brief goes to the inbox. Safe to re-run |
| `interviews.invite` | 13:53 daily | Invites verified authors whose dated release is 21–35 days out to answer interview questions in their dashboard (one invitation per author and book) | Safe to re-run. A missed day only delays invitations |
| `news.daily_roundup` | 10:30 daily | Builds and publishes "Today in LitRPG" (`/news/today`) from today's releases, the unreported tips, the last day's news briefs and the week ahead, then posts it to Bluesky and Mastodon if their secrets are set. A thin day (under `news.daily_min_items`) still publishes but stays out of search | Run it again from Automation: it does nothing if today's post exists. Social posts are made once per post (a KV marker) |
| `news.daily_send` | every 5 minutes, 11:00–13:55 | Emails today's roundup to Patch Notes Daily readers (`daily_digest`), `email.digest_chunk` a run, with the same kill switch, daily cap and circuit breaker as the weekly issue | A paused day stays paused; tomorrow starts fresh. Marketing mail waits until `email.postal_address` is set in production |
| `blog.weekly_roundup` | Monday 11:04 | Builds "New LitRPG & Progression Fantasy Releases: Week of …" when the week has at least `blog.min_books_per_roundup` books. It publishes straight away when `blog.auto_publish_roundups` is on; otherwise it waits a day in the inbox for a veto, then publishes itself | Safe to re-run: one post per week. A thin week is skipped |
| `blog.monthly_roundups` | 11:08 on the 1st and 15th | The 1st: the month's audiobooks and new series (book ones). The 15th: the most-followed dated releases of the next 60 days. Same publishing rule as the weekly roundup | Safe to re-run: one post per roundup and month |
| `inventory.generate` | 04:30 daily | Syncs the ad products and slots from the code (`ensureAdCatalog`) and makes inventory 120 days ahead: one unit per untargeted slot and day or week, with its price. Tag and book sponsor units are made when a campaign books them | Safe to re-run: existing units are kept. A miss only matters after 120 days; reserved house campaigns make their own units |
| `catalog.import` | every 5 minutes, and straight away after an upload | Ingests the next 20 rows of the oldest queued import, then queues itself again until the file is done | Failed rows are listed on the import's page with the reason; fix and re-upload just those rows (re-importing is safe: matches are updated, not duplicated) |

Every heartbeat also runs **inbox default actions** that are due. For item types whose change is already live (`tag_check`, `scope_check`), the default just closes them. For `quiz_ready`, it publishes the quiz. Author items (M6) and `post_review` (M7: a roundup or guest post publishes, or takes its calendar slot) run their handlers. The heartbeat also publishes scheduled posts whose time has come and queues their share cards, moves ad campaigns to live or completed on their dates, and releases unpaid inventory holds that have expired.

If the heartbeat itself stops (the dashboard shows "No heartbeat" or an old time), check the jobs Worker's cron trigger and logs in the Cloudflare dashboard.
